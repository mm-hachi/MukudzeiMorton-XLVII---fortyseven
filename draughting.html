Why drawing work stalls

  • Peaks outrun your team. A tender or a batch of submissions lands and your draughtsman is already full.
  • Submissions come back. A drawing that misses a regulation costs weeks in the municipal queue.
  • The site finds the mistakes. Drawing and site disagree, and the fix is most expensive once building has started.
  • New hires take months. Software, standards and office procedure all have to be learned before the work speeds up.
