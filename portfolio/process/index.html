Process

A clear brief. A useful handover.

A small, defined first step keeps the scope, files and decisions visible.

01

01 · Send the brief

Include the product list, CAD or photos, reference links, finish options, target platforms and deadline.

02

02 · Confirm scope

A written quote defines deliverables, revision rounds, file formats, milestones and what counts as new scope.

03

03 · Review checkpoints

Review the model, materials and data before the final exports are prepared.

04

04 · Handover

Receive named, organized files and a concise note on how to use them.

Working terms

  • Turnaround counts working days from deposit and a complete brief.
  • Every enquiry is acknowledged within 24 hours.
  • Written quotes are sent within 24 hours for standard work and within 48 hours for configurators.
  • Two revision rounds are included; extra rounds are billed hourly.
  • Packages under $500 are paid 100% upfront or through platform escrow. Larger projects use 50/50 payments; Configurator Pro uses 40/30/30 by milestone.
