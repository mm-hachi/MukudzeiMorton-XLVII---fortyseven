Dekara Designs · 3D showroom

One 3D model replaces photoshoots, samples and guesswork.

A traditional furniture site shows flat pictures and hopes customers can imagine the rest. Dekara lets them turn, resize, recolour and place a piece in their own room before they buy.

The problem with a 2D furniture site

  • Customers can't judge scale or fit. A photo doesn't say whether a sofa clears the doorway or fills the lounge. Wrong-size orders mean returns, remakes and unhappy reviews.
  • Every fabric needs its own photo. Most colour combinations never get shot, so customers buy blind or ask for samples.
  • Custom orders live in emails and calls. Sizes get misheard, quotes are slow, and the sale cools off while you wait.
  • Designers have to ask for files. Every request for models, swatches or specs is staff time.
  • New range, new shoot. Content dates quickly and is expensive to refresh.
